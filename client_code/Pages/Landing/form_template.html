Play every character yourself

Most motion-capture tools assume one actor per character. VRtoToon is built for the one-person studio: you record role after role, and the film keeps everyone in time.

From script to screen

How a picture gets made

Cast it

Pick from 25 toon actors (heroes, sidekicks, villains, critters) or dress your own variant in the wardrobe. Paste a script: names become your cast.

Perform in VR

Your head and hands become the character's, your voice moves its mouth. Teleprompter ahead, the director's monitor beside you. Passthrough works too.

Direct on your computer

Drag characters to make them walk, tap emotions and gestures onto the timeline, let the auto-director cut close-ups, two-shots and reactions.

Premiere it

Pick a film look, share a screening link, export a video, or take the whole scene to Blender for the final polish.

Questions

Which headsets work?

Any headset with a WebXR browser: Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S and Pro (in the Meta Quest Browser), Pico, and others. Nothing to install: open the site in the headset's browser. Passthrough works where the headset supports it.

I don't have a headset. Can I still make films?

Yes. The desk puppet lets you perform with the mouse and keyboard (your microphone still drives the mouth), and the voice booth records single lines. Characters without a recording still act, gesture and lip-flap to their lines.

How does one person play several characters?

Record one role at a time. While you play a character, everyone you already recorded plays back around you, and anyone not yet recorded reads their lines on your teleprompter. Each line is its own clip, so a retake only moves what comes after it.

Can I change the characters?

Today you can make variants in the wardrobe: colours, hair, hats, accessories, height and build. Characters are made of data, so more ways to change them are on the way.

What do I get when I export?

A video (MP4 or WebM, up to 1080p, widescreen, CinemaScope, square or vertical), subtitles, a dialogue WAV, and a Blender kit: the scene as glTF with animated characters, the set, the cameras and an import script that sets everything up.

Who owns what I make?

You do. The characters, sets, music and effects are made in code by VRtoToon, so you can publish your films anywhere. Your recordings stay private unless you share a screening link.

Is it free?

Yes, VRtoToon is free to use while it grows. Exports happen in your own browser, so they cost nothing to make.
